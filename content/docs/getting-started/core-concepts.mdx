Everything in Epilykos follows one path: **sources** read your equipment, each reading is stored as a
named **metric**, and **cards** on your dashboards show metrics.

## Sources

A source is one connection to a device or system: an inverter's Wi-Fi dongle, a Modbus link, a
Bluetooth battery, Home Assistant, an MQTT broker and so on. You add them in **Settings → Sources**.
Several source types hold more than one device, for example one card per dongle or battery pack.

For each source you choose which of its values to keep and what to call them. Sources with a device
profile (Modbus, RS232, WiFi dongle) fill in suggested names for you.

See [Data sources](/data-sources) for each type.

## Metrics

A metric is a named reading, such as `battery_soc` or `pv1_power`. The name is what connects your
hardware to the dashboard: a card shows whatever metric you pick on it.

### Naming conventions

- Use **lowercase** with **underscores**: `battery_voltage`, not `BatteryVoltage`.
- Be **descriptive**: `pv1_power` is better than `solar`.
- Use **consistent suffixes**: `_power`, `_voltage`, `_current`, `_energy`, `_soc`, `_temp`.
- Number multiple PV strings: `pv1_power`, `pv2_power`.

Bluetooth BMS readings are named for you as `bms_<name>_<key>`, for example `bms_pack1_voltage`.

### Metric roles

Roles tell Epilykos which of your metrics is which. The setup wizard fills them in; change them in
**Settings → Metrics → Metric roles**. They feed the history behind the power and energy charts, the
daily and monthly tables, the energy day, flows and costs cards, and savings.

| Role | Unit | Typical metric names |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solar power | W | `pv_power`, `solar_power` |
| Consumption (home use) | W | `load_power`, `ac_output_power` |
| Battery charge / discharge power | W | `battery_power` (one signed value fills both), or separate charge and discharge metrics |
| Grid import / export | W | `grid_power`, `grid_import`, `grid_export` |
| Battery SOC | % | `battery_soc` |
| Solar voltage | V | `pv_voltage`, `pv1_voltage` |
| Daily solar, consumption, battery charge and discharge, grid import and export | kWh today | `daily_solar`, `pv_energy_today` (reset at midnight by the inverter) |
| Generator power and daily generator energy | W, kWh today | `generator_power`, `generator_energy_today` (inverters with a generator input) |

No daily energy reading for a role? A [combined metric](/settings/metrics) can work it out from
power, or add several inverters' readings together.

## Cards and dashboards

A dashboard is a grid of cards. Each card shows one or more metrics as a flow diagram, gauge, chart,
table, forecast and so on. You can have several dashboards, for example "Overview", "Battery" and "TV
display", and choose which one opens on computers and which on phones. See
[Layout editor](/dashboard/editor).

## How often readings update

The main cycle runs every 30 seconds, and each source has its own read interval. Open dashboards get
new readings pushed to them as they arrive, without reloading. Charts and tables fill in as history
builds up.

Readings are kept in full for 30 days. After that they are kept as 5-minute averages. Text readings,
such as an inverter's mode, are not kept beyond 30 days.

## Who can see what

The dashboard is open to anyone who can reach it: no login. Settings, the layout editor, the card
showcase and the Controls page need the admin password. Changing a device setting needs the password
again. See [Security](/settings/security).
