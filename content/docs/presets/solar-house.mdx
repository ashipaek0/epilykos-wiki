![The Solar house dashboard: a house with solar panels in the middle, weather top left, the solar forecast and power rings on the right, and savings along the bottom](/images/preset-solar-house.webp)

A clean, mostly-transparent dashboard built around a background picture. The numbers float over the
house, and everything else sits at the edges so the picture stays the focus. It suits a wall display or a
tablet in landscape.

**[Download solar-house.json](/presets/solar-house.json)**

Then follow [Use a preset](/presets#use-a-preset).

## What's on it

- **Weather** (top left): temperature, feels like, humidity, wind, rain, UV, pressure, sunrise and sunset.
- **Solar forecast** (top right): today's expected energy, with tomorrow and the days after.
- **Solar** and **Load** (over the house): energy so far today.
- **Power rings** (right): production and consumption right now.
- **Battery** (bottom right): state of charge.
- **Savings** (bottom): today, this week, this month and this year.

## What it needs

| Card | Metric or setting |
| --- | --- |
| Solar | `pv_energy_today` |
| Load | `load_energy_today` |
| Power rings | `pv_total_power` and `load_total_power` (both scaled 0–8000 W) |
| Battery | `battery_soc_estimate` (the [Virtual Battery](/settings/metrics) estimate) |
| Weather and forecast | A location and forecast source in [Forecast and weather](/settings/forecast-and-weather) |
| Savings | [Prices and savings](/settings/prices-and-savings) set up |

If your metrics are named differently, open the card in the editor and choose yours. If you have a real
battery state of charge, use that for the Battery card instead of the estimate.

## Make it yours

- **Background picture:** the preset points at a picture hosted on an image site, so it loads from there.
  To use your own, open **Dashboard style…** and change the background image.
- **Ring scale:** the rings assume a system up to 8 kW. Open the ring card and change **max** to suit
  yours.
- **Layout:** it uses a fine, free-placement grid with overlap and *Scale to screen width* on, so it keeps
  its shape on different screens. See [Layout grid](/dashboard/editor#layout-grid) to change that.
