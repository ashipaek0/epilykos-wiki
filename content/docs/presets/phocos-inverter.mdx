A plain, readable dashboard for a Phocos Any-Grid system, laid out as a single column that fills the
screen. It starts with the live picture (grid, battery, solar and load around the inverter) and scrolls
down through the day's totals, the forecast, peak solar, the energy and power charts and daily and monthly
tables. It works well on a desktop browser, and the single column also reads well on a phone.

**[Download phocos-inverter.json](/presets/phocos-inverter.json)**

Then follow [Use a preset](/presets#use-a-preset).

## What's on it

- **System overview** with the flow diagram, the weather, and three extra readings: inverter mode, battery
  energy left and battery voltage.
- **Energy totals:** from the grid, consumption, solar, battery in and battery out.
- **Solar forecast** and **Peak solar** (the best power of each day, over 90 days).
- **Grid** card with a timeline, cost and daily hours, plus the prepaid meter line.
- **Daily energy chart**, **Energy of the day** and flows tabs, **Savings** and a **Power chart** with
  statistics.
- **Daily** and **monthly tables** of load, solar and grid use.

## What it needs

It expects the metrics a [Phocos Any-Grid profile](/data-sources/supported-hardware) provides:

| Card | Metric |
| --- | --- |
| Inverter mode | `inv2_operating_mode_label` |
| Battery voltage and energy | `battery_voltage`, `battery_energy` |
| Power chart | `load_total_power`, `pv_total_power`, `inv1_battery_net_power`, `grid_import` |
| Daily energy chart | `daily_solar`, `daily_grid_import`, `daily_consumption` |

The overview, totals, grid, savings and table cards use your
[metric roles](/getting-started/core-concepts#metric-roles), so set those first. Weather and forecast need
[Forecast and weather](/settings/forecast-and-weather), and savings and cost need
[Prices and savings](/settings/prices-and-savings).

## Make it yours

The file uses plain names (Grid, Home, Battery energy left) and has no savings start date or prepaid
wording. The screenshot above is from a real site that renamed some cards, so yours will read a little
differently. Then:

- **Names:** open the overview card to call the grid and the load whatever you like.
- **Inverter picture:** add your own in the overview card's settings. Without one it shows a plain
  inverter icon.
- **Prepaid meter:** the Grid card's prepaid line is off. Turn on the prepaid balance in the card if you use
  the [prepaid meter](/settings/prepaid-meter).
- **Inverter numbers:** the `inv1_` and `inv2_` metrics exist only for the units you have. Point a card at
  one that exists.
