Automations change your devices for you. Turn a switch on or off, or set a value, when something is
true: the battery is full, the sun is out, it's night.

Open **Automations** from the menu on the [Controls page](/dashboard/controls). You need to be signed in.

## How one works

An automation says what things should look like **while** its conditions are true, and, if you want,
what they should look like when they aren't.

> **When** battery is above 90% for 5 minutes, **then** turn on the geyser. **Otherwise** turn it off.

Conditions can be a reading (battery %, solar watts, a forecast number) or a time of day. Use all of
them, or any one of them.

An action can be several steps. Put them in one automation and they run in order, and go back together
in "otherwise".

## What you can change

The same things you can change on the Controls page: switches, numbers and choices on Tuya, Home
Assistant, MQTT, inverters and batteries. When you add a device, its settings show up here too. Raw
register writes are not available.

## Starting one

1. Unlock with your password (top of the page).
2. Choose **New automation**, fill it in, **Save**. It starts turned off.
3. Press **Test now** to see what it would do right now. Nothing is sent.
4. Turn it on.

## Good to know

- **It works even when "Allow device changes" is off.** That switch is for you pressing buttons.
  Automations are the exception, and every change they make is written to the change log.
- **No data, no action.** If a reading is missing or older than the limit you set (15 minutes to
  start), the automation waits.
- **It only writes when it has to.** If the device already matches, nothing is sent. If it doesn't, it
  tries again, up to four times, then tells you.
- **It keeps things the way you said.** If you change a setting by hand while its automation is
  running, it puts it back within a few minutes. To change it yourself, press **Take over** (for an
  hour, or until you resume).
- **Pause all** stops every automation at once. Starting again needs your password.
- **Tuya** switches can look right until the next poll, even if the plug ignored the command.
- **It is not protection.** If the grid drops and a switch fails, nothing else steps in. Your
  inverter's own low-battery cut-off is what protects the battery.

## Ideas

- **Water heater on spare sun:** battery above 90% and solar above 1500 W for 5 minutes → water heater
  on. Otherwise off.
- **Cheap-rate charging:** between 23:00 and 05:00 → turn on grid charging, set charge amps to 60.
  Otherwise grid charging off, amps back to 20.
- **Save the grid on sunny days:** forecast for tomorrow above 25 kWh → turn off grid use in the
  inverter.
- **Night pump:** between 18:00 and 20:00 on weekdays → pump on.

## If it didn't run

Open **Logs** at the bottom of the page. It lists every change and every failure with the reason.

A card that says *Waiting for data* is missing a reading: check the metric exists and is updating.
