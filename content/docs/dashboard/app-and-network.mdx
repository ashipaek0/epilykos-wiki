You can install Epilykos on a phone, tablet or computer and open it like any other app, with its own
icon and window. (Behind the scenes it's a Progressive Web App, so there's nothing to download from an
app store.)

## Install it

Open the dashboard in your browser, then:

- **On Android (Chrome)**, open the menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **On iPhone and iPad (Safari)**, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **On a computer (Chrome or Edge)**, click the install icon in the address bar.

On iPhone and iPad, [push notifications](/settings/notifications) only work once Epilykos has been
added to the Home Screen.

## What the app does

| Feature | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| Live updates | New readings appear on the open dashboard as soon as they arrive. |
| Works offline | The app's own files are kept on the device, so it opens even without a connection. |
| Background refresh | Where the browser allows it (Chrome does), the installed app asks to refresh readings about once a minute even when it's closed. The browser decides how often that really happens. |
| Quick start | The last dashboard you saw is kept on the device, so it appears instantly when you reopen the app. |
| Home and away addresses | The app tries your home address first and switches to your away address when it needs to. |

## Home and away addresses

Set these in **Settings → Network**:

- **Address at home**, for example `http://192.168.1.10:3000`. The app uses this while your phone or
  computer is on your home network: it's fast, and works even when the internet is down.
- **Address away from home**, for example `https://energy.example.com`. The app uses this everywhere
  else.

The app tries the home address first and switches to the away address if it can't reach it. Both
addresses are handed to the app when you save them, so open Settings and save them once on each device
you install the app on.

Don't have an address for when you're away? [Tailscale or a Cloudflare Tunnel](/settings/network-and-remote-access)
can give you one, and Tailscale even fills in **Address away from home** for you.

## Troubleshooting

If live updates stop working with the message *closed before connection is established*, an old copy
of the app's background worker is getting in the way. Clear it (in your browser's settings, under site
data for the Epilykos address) and reload the page. If you use a reverse proxy, also check its
[WebSocket settings](/settings/network-and-remote-access#reverse-proxy).
