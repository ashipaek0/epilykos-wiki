The Controls page, at `/controls`, is where you change inverter and battery settings, and where the
switch, selector, slider and knob cards live. You need to be signed in to use it.

![The Controls page, locked, with Device changes and Expert writes off](/images/controls.webp)

## How it keeps you safe

Changing an inverter's settings can affect your whole power system, so the Controls page is careful:

- **Device changes** starts switched off. While it's off, nothing on this page or on any dashboard can
  change a setting on your inverter or battery.
- Even when it's on, you have to enter your password again to **Unlock** the page. The unlock lasts
  5 minutes, and ends early when you sign out or press **Lock now**.
- Before changing anything, Epilykos reads the current value. It refuses the change if the new value
  is outside the setting's allowed range or step, if the device reports a current value that's out of
  range (a sign that the register might mean something different on your model), or if the value has
  changed since the page last showed it.
- After writing, Epilykos reads the setting back and tells you if it doesn't match what you asked for.
- Every attempt, including the refused ones, is recorded in the **Change log**.

<Callout type="warn">
  A changed setting changes how your inverter or battery behaves. Check each value against your
  device's manual, and change one thing at a time.
</Callout>

## Device settings

These devices can have settings changed so far:

| Device | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| **LuxPower** (GETA dongle, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) | Battery charge limits; on recognised models also the charging and discharging power limits, the grid charging power limit, the charging modes (grid charging, charge first, forced discharge) and three grid-charging windows |
| **JBD and JK Bluetooth BMS** | Charging and discharging on or off |
| **Phocos Any-Grid** (over Bluetooth) | A set of settings known to be safe, on the 3, 5, 6.5 and 8 kW models |

A couple of notes:

- **LuxPower:** the power limits, charging modes and charging windows are only offered when the
  inverter reports a model they apply to. That check follows what the LuxCloud app does; it isn't a
  guarantee for every unit, and the inverter's firmware may still refuse or limit a value. **Download
  verification** saves a read-only snapshot of the inverter's settings, which is handy to keep before
  and after a change.
- **JBD and JK:** switching discharging off cuts power to whatever the pack supplies, so the page asks
  you to confirm that separately. If a pack won't switch charging back on, it's usually protecting
  itself.

## Expert register writes

**Expert writes** (off by default) let you read and write any register number, for registers you've
checked in your device's manual. Register numbers can be decimal or hex (`0x…`), and values are whole
numbers from 0 to 65535 in the register's raw units, with no scaling. The same unlock and change log
apply.

## Control cards

To add control cards, open the [layout editor](/dashboard/editor), choose **Controls page** from the
dashboard menu, and add a **Toggle switch**, **Button**, **State select**, **Slider** or **Rotary
knob**. They appear on the Controls page under **Switches and selectors**.

The **slider** and the **knob** set a number: a Home Assistant `number` or `input_number`, a Tuya
value, or an MQTT topic. Pick what you want to control and give it a lowest value, highest value and
step (a Home Assistant number fills these in for you). Moving the control only previews the value; it's
sent when you let go of the slider, or stop turning the knob or pressing keys, so each gesture is one
entry in the change log. Tapping the knob changes nothing, and the arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home and
End work on both.

Control cards follow the same rules as everything else here: **Device changes** has to be on, and
each change needs the page to be unlocked.
