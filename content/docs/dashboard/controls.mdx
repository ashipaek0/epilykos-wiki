The Controls page, at `/controls`, is where inverter and battery settings are changed, and where the
switch, selector, slider and knob cards work. It needs sign-in.

![The Controls page, locked, with Device changes and Expert writes off](/images/controls.webp)

## Safety model

- **Device changes** is off until you turn it on. While it's off, nothing on the page or the
  dashboard can change an inverter or battery setting.
- Changes need your password again: **Unlock** lasts 5 minutes, and ends when you sign out or press
  **Lock now**.
- Each setting is read first. A change is refused when the new value is outside the setting's range
  or step, when the device reports a current value outside that range (the register may mean
  something else on your model), or when the value changed since the page showed it.
- After writing, the setting is read back, and a different value is reported.
- Every attempt, refused ones included, is kept in the **Change log**.

<Callout type="warn">
  A changed setting changes how your inverter or battery behaves. Check each value against your
  device's manual, and change one thing at a time.
</Callout>

## Device settings

Settings available so far:

| Device | What can be changed |
| --- | --- |
| **LuxPower** (GETA dongle, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) | Battery charge-level limits; on recognised models, charging and discharging power limits, grid charging power limit, charging-mode flags (grid charging, charge first, forced discharge) and three grid-charging windows |
| **JBD and JK Bluetooth BMS** | Charging and discharging on/off |
| **Phocos Any-Grid** (Bluetooth) | An allowlisted set of settings, for the 3, 5, 6.5 and 8 kW models |

Notes:

- **LuxPower:** the power limits, flags and charging windows are only offered when the inverter
  identifies as a model they apply to. That check follows the LuxCloud app; it isn't certification of
  every unit, and the firmware can still reject or limit a value. **Download verification** saves a
  read-only snapshot of the inverter's settings, useful before and after a change.
- **JBD and JK:** turning discharging off cuts what the pack powers, so the page asks you to confirm
  that separately. A pack that refuses to turn charging back on is usually protecting itself.

## Expert register writes

**Expert writes** (off by default) allow reading and writing any register number, for registers you
have checked in your device's manual. Register numbers are decimal or hex (`0x…`); values are whole
numbers from 0 to 65535 in raw register units, with no scaling. The same unlock and change log apply.

## Control cards

Add control cards in the [layout editor](/dashboard/editor): open the dashboard menu, pick **Controls
page**, and add a **Toggle switch**, **Button**, **State select**, **Slider** or **Rotary knob**.
They appear under **Switches and selectors**.

The **slider** and the **knob** set a number on a Home Assistant `number` or `input_number`, a Tuya
value, or an MQTT topic. Pick what to set and give its lowest value, highest value and step (a Home
Assistant number fills these in itself). Moving the control only shows the value; it's sent when you
let go of the slider, or stop turning the knob or pressing keys, so one gesture is one change in the
log. A tap on the knob changes nothing. Arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home and End work on both.

Control cards follow the same rules as everything else here: **Device changes** must be on, and a
change needs the unlock.
