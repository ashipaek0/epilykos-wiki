The Controls page, at `/controls`, is where you change inverter and battery settings, and where the
switch, selector, slider and knob cards live. You need to be signed in to use it.

![The Controls page, locked, with Device changes and Expert writes off](/images/controls.webp)

## How it keeps you safe

Changing an inverter's settings can affect your whole power system, so the Controls page is careful:

- **Device changes** starts switched off. While it's off, nothing on this page or on any dashboard can
  change a setting on your inverter or battery.
- Even when it's on, you have to enter your password again to **Unlock** the page. The unlock lasts
  5 minutes, and ends early when you sign out or press **Lock now**.
- Before changing anything, Epilykos reads the current value. It refuses the change if the new value
  is outside the setting's allowed range or step, if the device reports a current value that's out of
  range (a sign that the register might mean something different on your model), or if the value has
  changed since the page last showed it.
- After writing, Epilykos reads the setting back and tells you if it doesn't match what you asked for.
- Every attempt, including the refused ones, is recorded in the **Change log**.

<Callout type="warn">
  A changed setting changes how your inverter or battery behaves. Check each value against your
  device's manual, and change one thing at a time.
</Callout>

## Device settings

These devices can have settings changed so far:

| Device | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| **LuxPower** (dongle, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) | Battery charge limits; charging, discharging and grid charging current and power limits; export to the grid and its limit; the grid charging rule and windows; charge first and forced discharge with their limits and windows; output priority, AC input mode, eco and green mode, charge last, fast zero export and the buzzer |
| **JBD and JK Bluetooth BMS** | Charging and discharging on or off |
| **Phocos Any-Grid** (over Bluetooth) | A set of settings known to be safe, on the 3, 5, 6.5 and 8 kW models |

A couple of notes:

- **LuxPower:** the charging and discharging power limits, the grid charging power limit, the grid
  charging, charge-first and forced-discharge switches and the grid-charging windows come from the
  LuxCloud app and only work when the inverter reports a GETA model. The other settings follow the
  LuxPower protocol, so the **LuxPowerTek Generic Hybrid** profile offers them on any LuxPower
  inverter; the **LuxPowerTek GETA 3.6kW 24V** profile offers the ones that model has. The inverter's
  firmware may still refuse or limit a value. **Download verification** saves a read-only snapshot of
  the inverter's settings, which is handy to keep before and after a change.
- **JBD and JK:** switching discharging off cuts power to whatever the pack supplies, so the page asks
  you to confirm that separately. If a pack won't switch charging back on, it's usually protecting
  itself.

## Expert register writes

**Expert writes** (off by default) let you read and write any register number, for registers you've
checked in your device's manual. Register numbers can be decimal or hex (`0x…`), and values are whole
numbers from 0 to 65535 in the register's raw units, with no scaling. The same unlock and change log
apply.

## Control cards

To add control cards, open the [layout editor](/dashboard/editor), choose **Controls page** from the
dashboard menu, and add a **Toggle switch**, **Button**, **State select**, **Slider** or **Rotary
knob**. They appear on the Controls page under **Switches and selectors**.

### What a card can control

A card can control a Tuya value, a Home Assistant entity, an MQTT topic, or an **inverter or battery
setting** from the list under [Device settings](#device-settings). Device settings appear in the card's
picker under **Inverter and battery**, followed by the device's name, and each card offers only the
settings that suit it:

| Card | Inverter and battery settings it offers |
| --- | --- |
| **Toggle switch** | Settings that are on or off, such as grid charging, charge first and forced discharge on a LuxPower, or charging and discharging on a JBD or JK battery |
| **State select** | Settings with a list of choices, such as output priority. The choices are filled in for you |
| **Slider** and **Rotary knob** | Settings that are a number, such as a charging power limit. The lowest value, highest value, step and unit are filled in for you |

Times of day (such as the grid charging windows) have no card, and the **Button** card doesn't offer
device settings. Like every control card, these only work on the Controls page; on a dashboard they
show a link to it.

A card for a device setting reads the setting from the device when the page opens, once a minute while
the page stays open, and after each change. It isn't shown from the dashboard's regular readings, so
what it shows is what the device reports. Changing it follows the same checks as the list on this page:

- the current value is read first, and the change is refused if it no longer matches what the card
  showed (the card then shows what it is now);
- the value is read back afterwards, and every attempt goes in the **Change log**;
- if the device can't change that setting (for example, a setting for a model yours isn't), the card
  is locked and says why. If the device doesn't answer, the card shows as unavailable.

A card sends one request to the device each time it reads, so a page with many device cards makes the
dongle busy; a few cards that you really use work better than a card for every setting.

### Sliders and knobs

The **slider** and the **knob** set a number: a Home Assistant `number` or `input_number`, a Tuya
value, an MQTT topic, or a device setting. Pick what you want to control and give it a lowest value,
highest value and step (a Home Assistant number or a device setting fills these in for you). Moving the control only previews the value; it's
sent when you let go of the slider, or stop turning the knob or pressing keys, so each gesture is one
entry in the change log. Tapping the knob changes nothing, and the arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home and
End work on both.

Control cards follow the same rules as everything else here: **Device changes** has to be on, and
each change needs the page to be unlocked.
