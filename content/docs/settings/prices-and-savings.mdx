**Settings → Prices and savings** holds your energy prices, battery wear and how solar savings are
counted. All prices are per kWh, in your currency.

## Prices

![Settings → Prices and savings](/images/settings-prices.webp)

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Currency symbol** | For example €, $, £ or ₦. |
| **Grid buy price per kWh** | What you pay the grid for one kWh. Also used for solar savings. |
| **Grid sell price per kWh** | What the grid pays you for one exported kWh. Leave 0 if you aren't paid for export. |
| **Generator buy price per kWh** | What one kWh from your generator costs you, fuel and upkeep. For inverters with a generator input; leave 0 if you have none. |
| **Battery wear cost per kWh** | Charged on every kWh into or out of the battery. |

The **Costs and earnings** card uses these: grid costs and export earnings, generator costs (from the
daily generator role) and battery wear.

### Working out battery wear

Battery price ÷ (usable kWh × rated cycles × 2). For example, a battery that cost 3,000,000 with
15 kWh usable and 6,000 rated cycles: 3,000,000 ÷ (15 × 6,000 × 2) = 17 per kWh.

## Solar savings

Each day's saving is that day's solar kWh × a price; today, week (from Monday), month and all time
add the days up. **Solar savings** picks the price:

| Choice | Price per solar kWh | Example (4 kWh solar, grid 0.30, generator 0.50) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **At the grid buy price** (default) | The grid price | 4 × 0.30 = 1.20 |
| **By grid availability** | The grid price for solar made while the grid was on, the generator price for solar made during an outage. Needs the grid status metric; without it, the grid price. | Half made in an outage: 4 × (0.5 × 0.30 + 0.5 × 0.50) = 1.60 |
| **By what you actually bought** | The day's average price of the grid and generator energy you bought. A day with neither: the grid price. | Bought 2 kWh grid and 3 kWh generator: price 0.42, so 4 × 0.42 = 1.68 |

<Callout type="warn">
  Set a generator price before choosing either generator-based method. With a price of 0, solar made
  during outages (or the generator part of the average) counts as worth nothing.
</Callout>

Solar stored in the battery is valued when it was made, not when it was used.

**Solar energy metric** is the metric that counts solar energy produced today. Leave it empty to
detect it.

## Grid status

**Grid status metric** is the metric that says whether the grid is up: 1 or `on` while it is. It
drives the **Grid status** card, the grid outage and restored events, and savings by grid
availability.

## Battery

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Usable capacity (kWh)** | Used to project battery charge for the rest of the day. |
| **Lowest charge (%)** | The charge your inverter stops discharging at. |
