**Settings → Prepaid meter** is for a grid supply on a prepaid meter, where a token adds units to the meter and the
meter counts them down. One unit is one kWh. It is off by default: until you switch it on, nothing appears on the
dashboard, nothing is counted and nothing is stored.

## What Epilykos does, and what you do

Epilykos cannot buy a token, load one, or read the meter. It has no connection to either. You do those things by hand,
and the buttons only **record** what you did:

- **Record recharge**: after you have bought and loaded a token, enter the units it gave. You can also enter what you
  paid, and the price per unit is worked out.
- **Correct balance**: read the number off the meter and type it in. You can do this at any time, whenever the
  estimate has drifted from the meter. Epilykos sets the balance to your number, and it can learn from the difference.
- **Undo last entry**: takes back the latest recharge or correction, if you entered it by mistake.

Between those, Epilykos works out the balance itself. It takes the grid energy it measures off the balance:

> balance = last reading you gave + units you recorded since − grid energy used since

The first time, enter what the meter shows now (**Set the balance**) so there is a number to count down from.

## What it counts

The energy comes from the **daily grid import** counter (the metric mapped to that role on the
[Metrics](/settings/metrics) page). If there is no such counter, it uses the grid import power instead. If neither is
mapped, the page says so.

The meter and your inverter never agree exactly, so the balance is an estimate. When you correct it, Epilykos can use
the difference to work out how much the meter counts for each kWh it measures, so later estimates drift less. The
page shows what the last correction found, and the box **Use this to improve later estimates** in the dialog turns
that off for one correction.

## Warnings

Both are optional:

- **Warn when units left fall to**: a number of kWh.
- **Warn when days left fall to**: days left is the balance divided by your average grid use over the last 7 days.

When the balance reaches either level, Epilykos raises the event **Prepaid meter low**, and **Prepaid meter out of
units** when it reaches 0. They do nothing until you add an [event rule](/settings/notifications) for them, so you choose
where the message goes. Each is sent once, not on every poll, and again after the next recharge.

## Who can see the balance

The dashboard can be seen without signing in, so you choose:

- **Everyone who can see the dashboard** (the default): the balance, days left and what was used are shown on the cards,
  and published as metrics.
- **Only me, when signed in**: the balance is kept out of the published metrics, and the cards show nothing to anyone
  who is not signed in. Other cards and charts cannot show the balance either.

Recharges, corrections, the list of entries and these settings always need you to be signed in, whichever you pick.

## Where it shows

- The [Prepaid meter card](/dashboard/cards#prepaid-meter): the units left, a bar, days left, facts, the latest entries and the buttons.
- An optional strip on the [Grid status card](/dashboard/cards#prepaid-meter-on-the-grid-card).
- The metrics `prepaid_balance` (kWh), `prepaid_days_left`, `prepaid_used_since_recharge` (kWh) and
  `prepaid_cost_per_unit`, which any other card, chart or notification can use (unless the balance is private).

The entries are kept like your other settings, so a backup and restore keeps them.
