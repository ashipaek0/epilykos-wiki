**Settings → Forecast and weather** sets up the solar production forecast and the weather for your
location. Turn on **Show the solar forecast** to use them.

## Location

Enter your **Latitude** and **Longitude**, or press **Use my location** to take them from the browser.

## Panels

Add each group of panels that has its own size, tilt or direction, for example an east and a west
roof. The forecast works each one out and adds them up.

![Location and panels in Settings → Forecast and weather](/images/settings-forecast.webp)

For each array:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Array name** | For example "East roof". |
| **Peak capacity** | The array's size in kWp. |
| **Tilt** | In degrees: 0 is flat, 90 vertical. |
| **Facing** | The direction the panels face, in degrees: 180 is south, 90 east. |
| **Solcast resource ID** | Optional: this array's own rooftop site in Solcast. |

**Loss factor** is the share of rated output you actually get, from 0 to 1. 0.9 suits most systems.
**Installation date** is optional.

## Providers

| Provider | Accuracy | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open-Meteo** | Moderate: a general area forecast | Free, no key |
| **Solcast** | High: rooftop-level modelling | Free hobbyist plan, about 10 calls a day |

**Forecast provider** and **Weather provider** can each be **Auto** (Solcast when it's set up, else
Open-Meteo), **Solcast** or **Open-Meteo**.

For Solcast, create a rooftop site in the Solcast toolkit and enter your **Solcast API key** and
**Solcast resource ID**. If one site covers all your arrays, put its ID here; otherwise give each
array its own above. Epilykos keeps each Solcast forecast for 3 hours, about 8 calls a day, which fits
the free plan.

Press **Test forecast** to check it.

## When the forecast is unavailable

If Solcast's daily quota runs out or a provider can't be reached, Epilykos sends the **Solar forecast
unavailable** event. You can be told about it in [Notifications](/settings/notifications).

## Where the forecast shows up

The [forecast and weather cards](/dashboard/cards#forecast-and-weather), **Day totals**, **Energy
day**, the Telegram `/forecast` command and the scheduled forecast report, such as a "low solar
tomorrow" warning.
