**Settings → Uploads** sends your data to [PVOutput.org](https://pvoutput.org), a free service for
sharing and comparing solar output.

## Connect your PVOutput system

<Steps>
<Step>

In PVOutput, open **Settings**: turn on **API Access**, and note your **API Key** and your system's
**System Id**.

</Step>
<Step>

In Epilykos, turn on **Upload to PVOutput** and enter the **API key** and **System ID**.

</Step>
<Step>

Press **Test connection**. It also detects the **Time zone**; enter it yourself if needed, for
example `Africa/Lagos`.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **Upload every** 5, 10 or 15 minutes (match your PVOutput system's status interval), and enter
the **System size** in W.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Metric mapping

Pick the Epilykos metric for each PVOutput value:

| PVOutput value | What to map |
| --- | --- |
| **v1 Energy Generated** | Cumulative daily solar generation, for example `daily_solar_kwh`. |
| **v2 Power Generated (W)** | Solar output in watts, for example `solar_power`. |
| **v3 Energy Consumed** | Cumulative daily consumption, for example `daily_consumption`. |
| **v4 Power Consumed (W)** | Load in watts, for example `load_power`. |
| **v5 Temperature (°C)** | Ambient or inverter temperature. |
| **v6 Voltage (V)** | Grid or mains voltage. |

Epilykos energy metrics are in kWh; the unit selector next to v1 and v3 sets what PVOutput receives.

## Upload mode

- **Cumulative (c1)**: daily running totals. Right for most inverters.
- **Net data (n)**: v2 and v4 are net export and import. Only if your inverter reports net metering
  values.

## Upload queue and backfill

Uploads that fail (no internet, PVOutput down) wait in the **Upload queue** and are sent later. **View
queue** shows what's waiting. **Run backfill** finds gaps and uploads them. On a free PVOutput account
each status is sent on its own, so a large backlog can take several hours.

## PVOutput alerts

PVOutput can send its notifications to Epilykos. Enter a **Webhook URL** such as
`https://energy.example.com/api/pvoutput/webhook` here, and use the same URL as the notification
callback in your PVOutput settings. It has to be a public address that PVOutput's servers can reach;
see [Network and remote access](/settings/network-and-remote-access).

The address only accepts calls while uploads are on, for your own system ID, and at most 10 a minute.
Alerts arrive as the **PVOutput alert received** event in [Notifications](/settings/notifications).
