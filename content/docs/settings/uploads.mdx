**Settings → Uploads** can send your figures to [PVOutput.org](https://pvoutput.org), a free site where
people share and compare how their solar systems are doing.

## Connect your PVOutput system

<Steps>
<Step>

In PVOutput, open **Settings**, switch on **API Access**, and make a note of your **API Key** and your
system's **System Id**.

</Step>
<Step>

In Epilykos, switch on **Upload to PVOutput** and enter the **API key** and **System ID**.

</Step>
<Step>

Press **Test connection**. It also fills in the **Time zone** for you; if it doesn't, type it in, for
example `Africa/Lagos`.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **Upload every** 5, 10 or 15 minutes to match your PVOutput system's status interval, and enter
the **System size** in watts.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Metric mapping

Pick which Epilykos metric to send for each PVOutput value:

| PVOutput value | What to choose |
| --- | --- |
| **v1 Energy Generated** | The solar energy produced so far today, for example `daily_solar_kwh`. |
| **v2 Power Generated (W)** | Solar power in watts, for example `solar_power`. |
| **v3 Energy Consumed** | The energy used so far today, for example `daily_consumption`. |
| **v4 Power Consumed (W)** | The house's power use in watts, for example `load_power`. |
| **v5 Temperature (°C)** | The outdoor or inverter temperature. |
| **v6 Voltage (V)** | The grid or mains voltage. |

Epilykos works in kWh; the unit selector next to v1 and v3 decides what PVOutput receives.

## Upload mode

- **Cumulative (c1)** sends running totals for the day. That's right for most inverters.
- **Net data (n)** treats v2 and v4 as net export and import. Only choose it if your inverter reports
  net-metering values.

## Upload queue and backfill

If an upload fails, because the internet is down or PVOutput is busy, it waits in the **Upload queue**
and is sent later. **View queue** shows what's waiting, and **Run backfill** looks for gaps and fills
them in. On a free PVOutput account each reading is sent separately, so a big backlog can take several
hours to clear.

## PVOutput alerts

PVOutput can send its notifications on to Epilykos. Enter a **Webhook URL** such as
`https://energy.example.com/api/pvoutput/webhook` here, and use the same address as the notification
callback in your PVOutput settings. It has to be a public address that PVOutput's servers can reach;
see [Network and remote access](/settings/network-and-remote-access).

That address only accepts calls while uploads are switched on, only for your own system ID, and at
most 10 a minute. Alerts arrive as the **PVOutput alert received** event, which you can pass on in
[Notifications](/settings/notifications).
