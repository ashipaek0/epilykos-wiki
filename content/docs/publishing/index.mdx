[Data sources](/data-sources) bring data **into** Epilykos. This section is the other way round: ways to
get your Epilykos data **out**, so other apps can use it.

## Ways to publish your data

| You want to | How | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See your Epilykos metrics as sensors in Home Assistant | Home Assistant reads them over your network | [Epilykos in Home Assistant](/publishing/home-assistant) |
| Share your solar figures on PVOutput.org | Epilykos uploads them | [Uploads (PVOutput)](/settings/uploads) |
| Send alerts to another app or service | Webhook and other notification channels | [Notifications](/settings/notifications) |

More ways will be added to this section over time.
