The **Battery management (BMS)** source reads the battery's own management system: state of charge,
cell voltages, temperatures and current. Packs can be read over Bluetooth or a wire, and several packs
can be combined into one battery bank.

## Bluetooth BMS

Bluetooth support is built in and uses the host's Bluetooth adapter. On Docker, see
[Install with Docker](/getting-started/install-docker#bluetooth) for what the host needs.

Decoding uses the [aiobmsble](https://pypi.org/project/aiobmsble/) library, which covers JK, JBD,
Daly, Seplos, ANT, Renogy, EG4, PACE and many more.

<Steps>
<Step>

In **Settings → Sources**, press **+ Add Bluetooth BMS** and give it a name.

</Step>
<Step>

Close the vendor's phone app, then press **🔍 Scan** and pick the pack. Recognised packs are labelled
with their type.

</Step>
<Step>

Press **Test Connection**, then **📥 Load BMS Metrics** and choose the readings to keep. Then
**Save changes**.

</Step>
</Steps>

Readings are stored as `bms_<name>_<key>`, for example `bms_pack1_voltage`, `bms_pack1_current`,
`bms_pack1_battery_level`, `bms_pack1_cell_voltage_1` and `bms_pack1_temp_1`.

If a rebranded pack is found by the scan but not recognised, set its **type** (JBD, JK, Daly, Seplos,
ANT, PACE / PACEEX) next to the MAC address. Leave it on auto-detect otherwise.

Things to know:

- Most packs accept **one Bluetooth connection at a time**. Close the vendor app while Epilykos is
  connected.
- All Bluetooth devices share one adapter and are read one after another: allow about 10 seconds per
  device in their read intervals.
- Bluetooth LE reaches about 10 m. On a Raspberry Pi 3, a USB Bluetooth adapter is more reliable than
  the onboard radio.
- JBD and JK packs can also have charging and discharging switched on and off from the
  [Controls page](/dashboard/controls).

## Wired BMS

**Wired BMS** connects over RS-485 or RS-232 through a USB adapter. Press **+ Add wired BMS**, pick the
serial port and a profile; the serial settings follow the profile.

| Profile | Packs | Wiring | Serial |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| JBD / Jiabaida Smart BMS | JBD, Jiabaida, Xiaoxiang, Overkill Solar and rebrands | UART port (TX, RX, GND) through a USB-TTL adapter, or the RS485 port (A, B) on boards that have one | 9600 8N1, no unit ID |
| JK-BMS | JK-B / JK-BD boards | JK RS485 adapter in the GPS port, A/B to a USB-RS485 adapter; or GPS-port TX/RX/GND to a USB-TTL adapter | 115200 8N1, no unit ID |
| PACE BMS (protocol 25) | Jakiper, Easun, Tewaycell, Greenrich, FSP, Eenovance and many rebrands | The pack's RS232 port (usually RJ11 / RJ12) or its RS485 port | 9600 8N1 (a few packs use 19200); set the pack address to its DIP-switch address, usually 1 |
| Cworth CE-H6K / CE-LBW-48100C | Cworth PACE packs | COMM port pin 7 = RS485-A, pin 8 = RS485-B, nothing else | Modbus-RTU, 9600 8N1 |

<Callout type="error" title="Never connect the BMS's power pin">
  - **JBD:** don't connect the UART port's power pin (often +12 V / VBAT) to the adapter.
  - **JK:** don't connect the GPS port's VBAT pin to the adapter.
  - **Cworth:** pin 3 carries +5 V. Connect only pins 7 and 8, and don't use a straight-through
    8-pin cable.
</Callout>

More notes:

- **JBD:** the Bluetooth module and the wired port usually share one UART. Unplug the Bluetooth module
  to use the wire, or use Bluetooth instead.
- **PACE:** if the pack doesn't answer, try its other port. On many packs the RS485 / CAN port is set
  to an inverter protocol. Older protocol 20 packs (for example EG4 LifePower4) are not covered.
- **JK:** the newer JK-PB inverter BMS speaks a different protocol and is not covered.
- **Cworth:** only one pack can be read per RS485 adapter: the packs default to address 0 and there's
  no documented way to change it over the wire. Its current is negative while charging, the opposite
  of the other profiles (positive = charging).

Wired JBD and JK packs report the same metric names as over Bluetooth (`voltage`, `current`,
`battery_level`, `cell_voltage_N`, `temp_N`), so dashboards and banks work with either connection.

## Battery banks

A battery bank combines several packs into one battery with computed readings. Press **+ Add battery
bank**, name it, and tick the packs in it. **Capacity (Ah)** is optional: leave it blank to use the
capacity each BMS reports.

**Add the usual battery metrics** adds these in one go:

| Reading | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| `soc` | Charge, weighted by capacity |
| `voltage` | Average |
| `current` | Add up |
| `power` | Add up |
| `temperature` | Highest |
| `cell_voltage_min` | Lowest |
| `cell_voltage_max` | Highest |

You can add your own with **+ Add metric**. The calculations are **Add up**, **Average**, **Lowest**,
**Highest**, **Charge, weighted by capacity**, **Add up, weighted by capacity** and **Newest
reading**. Weighted calculations use each pack's capacity: its Ah override, or what it reports. Each
bank reading is saved with `bank_` in front of its name. A bank with one pack passes its readings
through unchanged.

Press **Test bank** to check the result before saving.
