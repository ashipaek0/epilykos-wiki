The **Tuya** source reads Tuya and Smart Life plugs, meters and switches directly over your own
network (encrypted, on port 6668). The Tuya cloud is only needed once, to fetch each device's local
key; after that, everything stays local.

## Set it up with your Smart Life account

<Steps>
<Step>

Find your **Smart Life UID**: in the Smart Life app, go to **Me → ⚙ → Account → User ID**.

</Step>
<Step>

In **Settings → Sources → Tuya**, enter the UID under **Smart Life login** and press **Generate QR
code**. Scan the code with the Smart Life app (**Me → + → Scan QR Code**).

</Step>
<Step>

Your devices appear with everything filled in: their local keys, the names of their data points (DPs)
and their addresses on your network.

</Step>
<Step>

Use the dropdowns to give the data points you care about a metric name.

</Step>
<Step>

Press **Verify all devices** to test them all at once, then **Save changes**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Add a device by hand

If the cloud login didn't find a device, press **+ Add device manually** and enter its **Device ID**,
**Local Key** and **IP Address**, then add data points with **+ Add DP Mapping**.

## Discovery on Docker

The cloud login fills in the keys and data points, but each device's address on your network comes
from discovery: Tuya devices announce themselves with broadcasts on your local network. Those
broadcasts only reach a Docker container that uses host networking on a Linux host, so switch on
`network_mode: "host"` in the compose file if you want them (see
[Install with Docker](/getting-started/install-docker#tuya-discovery)). Otherwise, including on Docker
Desktop for Windows and macOS, enter each device's IP address yourself. EpilykosOS needs nothing
extra.

## Controlling Tuya devices

Switch and selector cards, and the slider and knob, can change Tuya values from the
[Controls page](/dashboard/controls).
