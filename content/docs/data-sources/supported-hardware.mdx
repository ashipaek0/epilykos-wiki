## Bluetooth BMS

Bluetooth battery packs don't use profiles. Scan for them in the **Battery management (BMS)** source: packs the
decoder library recognises (JK, JBD, Daly, Seplos, ANT, Renogy, EG4, PACE and many more) are labelled
with their type. See [Battery BMS](/data-sources/bms).
