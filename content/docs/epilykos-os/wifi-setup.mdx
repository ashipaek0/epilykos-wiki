The box needs to be on your network before you can open its dashboard. With an Ethernet cable there's
nothing to set up: just plug it in. For Wi-Fi, pick whichever of the three ways below suits you.

<Callout title="2.4 GHz only">
  A Raspberry Pi 3B can only see 2.4 GHz networks. It also won't see a router on channel 12 or 13
  until it knows which country it's in. Set the country in Raspberry Pi Imager or in `wifi.txt`;
  otherwise the box takes it from the time zone you choose during setup, which only happens after
  Wi-Fi is working.
</Callout>

## With Raspberry Pi Imager

Add EpilykosOS to Raspberry Pi Imager's list using this repository URL:

```text
https://github.com/ashipaek0/epilykos-os/releases/latest/download/epilykos-os-imager.json
```

Then choose EpilykosOS, and enter your Wi-Fi network and Wi-Fi country in Imager's settings before
you write the card.

## With a wifi.txt file

<Steps>
<Step>

Once the card is written, open its boot drive on your computer. You'll find a file there called
`wifi-example.txt`.

</Step>
<Step>

Make a copy of it in the same folder, name the copy `wifi.txt`, and fill it in:

```text title="wifi.txt"
ssid=Home Net
password=your-wifi-password
country=NG
hidden=no
```

| Line | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| `ssid` | Your network's name, exactly as your phone shows it. |
| `password` | Its password, 8 to 63 characters. Leave it empty for an open network. |
| `country` | The two-letter code of the country the box is in, such as GB, US, NG or DE. |
| `hidden` | `yes` if your network doesn't broadcast its name. |

</Step>
<Step>

Put the card back in the Pi and power it on.

</Step>
</Steps>

The box reads `wifi.txt` once, saves the network, and then deletes the file so your password doesn't
stay on the card.

## From your phone over Bluetooth

EpilykosOS supports [Improv Wi-Fi](https://www.improv-wifi.com), so you can give it your Wi-Fi details
over Bluetooth:

1. On a phone or computer with Chrome, go to [improv-wifi.com](https://www.improv-wifi.com).
2. Choose **Connect device to Wi-Fi** and pick **Epilykos**.
3. Choose your network and enter its password.

The box opens Bluetooth setup by itself when it has been without a network for 2 minutes. There's no
need to pair the Pi in your computer's Bluetooth menu; setup doesn't use pairing.

## Change Wi-Fi later

In **Settings → Network → Wi-Fi**, press **Change Wi-Fi** to open Bluetooth setup, then connect from
improv-wifi.com as above. If you need longer, **Keep setup open 15 more minutes** gives you more time.

## Can't find the box?

- Try `http://epilykos.local:3000`. If your device can't open `.local` names, use the IP address your
  router gave the Pi instead.
- If devices on your network can't reach each other at all, the router is keeping them apart: turn off
  its "AP isolation" or "client isolation" setting and restart it. Connecting the Pi with an Ethernet
  cable also gets around it.
