Epilykos is software you run yourself, on your own box or server. The project does not run a service that
your readings are sent to, has no user accounts, and receives none of your data.

*Last updated: 13 November 2026.*

## What stays on your box

Your readings, history, settings, layouts, saved passwords and tokens are stored in a database on the
device where you installed Epilykos. They are not sent to the Epilykos project or to anyone else unless you
choose to connect a service, as described below.

## Services you choose to connect

Epilykos talks to a service only when you set one up, and sends it only what that service needs. For
example, your Solcast key and rooftop details go to Solcast for a forecast, a Telegram bot token goes to
Telegram to send you a message, and your Home Assistant address and token go to your Home Assistant. Each of
these is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

Epilykos also checks for new versions from the project's public update feed. That request carries no
personal data from your box, though the host of the feed (GitHub) sees it as it would any web request.

The dashboard and settings pages load their fonts, scripts and styles from the box itself, and Epilykos
includes no advertising or analytics trackers.

## Backups to other places

Epilykos can send a backup of your database to a folder you choose, and, if you connect it, to your own
Google Drive. The copy is locked on your box before it leaves: Epilykos encrypts the whole file with a
passphrase you choose. The passphrase is not sent anywhere and is not part of the file, so the storage
provider sees only encrypted data and the project cannot read it.

## Google user data

This section applies only if you connect your Google account to back up to Google Drive. It is optional,
and Epilykos works fully without it.

- **What Epilykos asks for.** Permission to see and manage only the files Epilykos itself creates in your
  Drive, in a folder called Epilykos. It does not ask to read your other files, your email, your contacts
  or your calendar, and it cannot see them.
- **What it accesses.** The encrypted backup files it created, and the Epilykos folder that holds them.
  It does not read your name, email address or any other profile detail.
- **How it uses it.** To upload an encrypted backup, to list the backups in that folder so you can choose
  one to restore, to download the one you choose, and to delete older backups so only the number you chose
  is kept. It does nothing else with your Google account.
- **Where it is stored.** The sign-in token Google gives your box is stored on your own box, locked with
  the box's own key. The backup files are stored in your own Google Drive. The project has no server and
  receives neither.
- **Sharing.** Information from your Google account is not shared with, sold to or given to anyone, and
  the project does not receive it.
- **Advertising and AI.** It is not used for advertising, profiling, or to develop or train any
  generalised AI or machine-learning model.
- **Human access.** Nobody at the project can read your Google data, because it never reaches us. The
  backup files are encrypted, so even someone with access to your Drive sees only unreadable data.
- **How long it is kept.** The sign-in token stays on your box until you disconnect Google Drive or delete
  the box. Backup files stay in your Drive until Epilykos replaces them with newer ones, or you delete them.
- **Stopping and deleting.** Switch remote backup off in **Settings → Backup and restore** to stop all
  uploads. To withdraw Epilykos's access to your Google account, remove it at
  [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), then delete the Epilykos
  folder in Drive if you no longer want the copies.

Epilykos's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Your control

You can export or delete your data at any time. A backup file is the whole database, and deleting the
database or uninstalling Epilykos removes everything it holds. Nothing is retained by the project, because
the project never receives it.
