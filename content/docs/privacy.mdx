Epilykos is software you run yourself, on your own box or server. The project does not run a service that
your readings are sent to, has no user accounts, and receives none of your data.

*Last updated: 13 November 2026.*

## What stays on your box

Your readings, history, settings, layouts, saved passwords and tokens are stored in a database on the
device where you installed Epilykos. They are not sent to the Epilykos project or to anyone else unless you
choose to connect a service, as described below.

## Services you choose to connect

Epilykos talks to a service only when you set one up, and sends it only what that service needs. For
example, your Solcast key and rooftop details go to Solcast for a forecast, a Telegram bot token goes to
Telegram to send you a message, and your Home Assistant address and token go to your Home Assistant. Each of
these is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

Epilykos also checks for new versions from the project's public update feed. That request carries no
personal data from your box, though the host of the feed (GitHub) sees it as it would any web request.

The dashboard and settings pages load their fonts, scripts and styles from the box itself, and Epilykos
includes no advertising or analytics trackers.

## Backups to other places

Epilykos can send a backup of your database to a folder you choose, and, if you connect it, to Google Drive.

- **The copy is locked on your box before it leaves.** Epilykos encrypts the whole file with a passphrase
  you choose. The passphrase is not sent anywhere and is not part of the file, so the storage provider sees
  only encrypted data and the project cannot read it.
- **Only the files Epilykos creates.** When you connect Google Drive, Epilykos asks for the permission that
  lets it see only files it creates itself, in a folder called Epilykos. It cannot see, read or change any
  of your other Drive files.
- **What it does with Google Drive.** It uploads the encrypted backup, lists the backups in that folder so
  you can restore one, downloads one when you restore, and deletes older backups to keep the number you
  chose. It does nothing else with your Google account.
- **Where the sign-in is kept.** The token Google gives your box is stored on your box, locked with the
  box's own key. It is never sent to the project.
- **No sharing and no advertising.** Information from your Google account is not shared with anyone,
  not sold, and not used for advertising, profiling or to train any model.
- **Stopping.** Switch the backup off in **Settings → Backup and restore** to stop all uploads. To
  withdraw Epilykos's access to your Google account, remove it at
  [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), then delete the Epilykos
  folder in Drive if you no longer want the copies.

## Your control

You can export or delete your data at any time. A backup file is the whole database, and deleting the
database or uninstalling Epilykos removes everything it holds. Nothing is retained by the project, because
the project never receives it.
