Epilykos is a dashboard for your solar system, inverter, batteries and smart home that runs on your
own hardware. It talks to your equipment directly, whether that's an inverter's Wi-Fi dongle, a
Modbus or RS232 cable, a Bluetooth battery, Home Assistant, MQTT, Tuya or a web API. It keeps the
history on your own machine, and shows everything live on a dashboard that anyone at home can open.
Settings stay behind an admin password.

You can run it on a Raspberry Pi with **EpilykosOS**, or with **Docker** on any system that runs it:
Linux, Windows, macOS or a NAS.

## Features

### Reads your equipment directly

Epilykos connects to inverter Wi-Fi dongles (Solarman V5, LuxPower, Growatt, Felicity), to Modbus over
RS-485 or the network, to RS232 inverters (Voltronic, Victron VE.Direct, SolaX, Phocos), and to battery
BMSes over Bluetooth or a cable. It can also pull readings from Home Assistant, MQTT, Tuya devices on
your network, and any web API that returns JSON. [Supported hardware](/data-sources/supported-hardware)
has the full list.

### A live dashboard anyone can open

Choose from more than 20 kinds of card, from animated energy flows to charts, tables, gauges and
forecasts, all refreshing every 30 seconds. You can build several dashboards and pick which one opens
on a computer and which on a phone. Nobody needs to sign in just to look.

### Device controls with safety checks

Supported inverters and batteries can have their settings changed from the Controls page. Every change
is checked against the setting's allowed range, read back from the device to confirm it took, and
written to a change log.

### Forecasts, prices and savings

See tomorrow's solar forecast from Open-Meteo (free) or Solcast, the weather, what your grid and
generator power cost, how much wear you're putting on the battery, and how much your solar has saved
you. Epilykos can also upload your figures to PVOutput.org.

### Alerts where you'll see them

Get push notifications, Telegram messages (the bot even answers `/status` and `/today`), Discord posts,
ntfy alerts or webhooks. Be told when a reading crosses a line or the grid goes off, or get a summary
every evening.

### Reach it from anywhere

Connect Tailscale or a Cloudflare Tunnel straight from Settings, without forwarding any ports. Install
Epilykos on your phone like an app, and it switches between your home and away addresses by itself.

### Safe to leave running

Your admin password can have a second step, saved passwords are encrypted, the database is snapshotted
every day, you can download backups, and updates roll themselves back if the new version doesn't start
properly.

### EpilykosOS

EpilykosOS turns a Raspberry Pi into an Epilykos appliance: flash it, power it on and finish setup in
your browser. After that it looks after its own updates and recovery.
