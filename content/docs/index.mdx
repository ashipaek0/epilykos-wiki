Epilykos is a self-hosted, real-time dashboard for solar systems, inverters, batteries and home
automation. It reads your equipment directly (Wi-Fi dongles, Modbus, RS232, Bluetooth, Home
Assistant, MQTT, Tuya and REST APIs), keeps the history in a database on your own machine, and shows
it on a dashboard anyone on your network can open. Settings stay behind an admin password.

Run it on a Raspberry Pi with **EpilykosOS**, or with **Docker** on any Linux computer.

## Features

### Reads your equipment directly

Inverter Wi-Fi dongles (Solarman V5, LuxPower, Growatt, Felicity), Modbus over RS-485 or TCP, RS232
inverters (Voltronic, Victron VE.Direct, SolaX, Phocos), battery BMS over Bluetooth or a wired link,
Home Assistant, MQTT, Tuya devices on your LAN, and any JSON API. See
[Supported hardware](/data-sources/supported-hardware).

### A live dashboard anyone can open

More than 20 card types, from energy-flow diagrams to charts, tables, gauges and forecasts, updated
every 30 seconds. Build several dashboards, with separate layouts for computers and phones. Viewing
needs no login.

### Device controls with safety checks

Change supported inverter and battery settings from the Controls page. Every change is checked
against the setting's range, read back from the device and kept in a change log.

### Forecasts, prices and savings

Solar forecasts from Open-Meteo (free) or Solcast, weather, grid and generator prices, battery wear
cost, and what your solar has saved you. Uploads to PVOutput.org.

### Alerts where you'll see them

Push notifications, Telegram (with `/status` and `/today` commands), Discord, ntfy or a webhook. Alert
on any reading, on events such as a grid outage, or send a daily summary.

### Reach it from anywhere

Tailscale or a Cloudflare Tunnel from Settings, with no port forwarding. Install it on your phone as
an app that switches between your home and away addresses by itself.

### Safe to leave running

Admin password with optional two-step sign-in, saved passwords encrypted at rest, daily snapshots,
downloadable backups, and one-click updates that roll back if the new version doesn't start.

### EpilykosOS

An operating system for the Raspberry Pi that runs Epilykos like an appliance: flash it, power it on,
finish setup from a browser. It updates and recovers by itself.
